# Sugar Nails Creative Brief

Status: Authored from the user's supplied brand facts, logo, palette, and five layout references.

## 1. Vibe and references

- User evidence: premium nail layout references with editorial serif typography, glass surfaces, arched media, rounded cards, soft animation, and a detailed footer.
- Authored direction: sweet, polished, warm, gentle, modern.

## 2. Scroll journey

1. Recognition: Sugar Nails feels warm, distinctive, and easy to book.
2. Trust: gentle care and personal style belong together.
3. Choice: visitors quickly understand the service range.
4. Atmosphere: the salon feels calm, welcoming, and considered.
5. Visit: location, hours, phone, and social links are effortless to find.
6. Commitment: the visitor books an appointment.

## 3. Energy curve

- Hero: bright anticipation, caused by a sunlit manicure and oversized editorial promise.
- Story: calm reassurance, caused by warm whitespace and a measured split composition.
- Services: playful curiosity, caused by lateral browsing and the moving sugar trail.
- Experience: quiet immersion, caused by a full-width salon scene and restrained overlay copy.
- Visit: practical confidence, caused by clear contact and hours cards.
- Close: warm decisiveness, caused by one resolved orange booking field.

## 4. Feeling and peak

- The visitor should move from delighted recognition to reassurance, curiosity, calm, confidence, and action.
- Peak: “It’s the nail salon site where the sweet orange trail guides you from choosing a look to booking it.”
- Peak location: Services gallery.

## 5. Signature move

- A sugar trail progresses across the services rail as cards move and completes as a candy-dot flourish beside the booking action.

## 6. Aesthetic range

- Premium editorial with a playful confectionery edge. Not maximalist, not sterile minimalism.

## 7. World structure

- Distinct scenes connected by recurring cream surfaces, burnt-orange rules, candy shapes, and rounded frames.
- Grammar: editorial confectionery chapters.
- Navigation: compact translucent header with a persistent Book Appointment action.
- Ending: a saturated booking close followed by practical footer details.
- Bans: no section counters, no generic scroll prompt, no invented claims or numbers, no repeated centered composition.

## 8. Available assets

- User-supplied transparent Sugar Nails logo.
- User-supplied palette: `#ea661c`, `#ffeac8`.
- Three project-specific generated photographs: hero manicure, service manicure, salon interior.
- Business details, booking link, Facebook, and Instagram supplied by the user.

## Visitor belief

## Primary action

**Book Appointment**

## Completed tell-someone sentence

It’s the site where a bright sugar-orange trail turns choosing your next nails into a gentle path to booking.

## Page grammar and score

| Beat | Device family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Layered parallax hero | The image, candy shapes, logo, and foreground move independently to create immediate depth. |
| Trust | Flow + reveal | The slower editorial split gives the promise room to feel credible. |
| Choice | Native horizontal pan + signature trail | Lateral movement naturally communicates range and remains touch-accessible. |
| Atmosphere | Full-bleed reveal | One immersive scene creates the emotional high after the playful gallery. |
| Visit | Staggered flow | Practical facts arrive clearly without competing motion. |
| Commitment | Pointer spotlight + resolved hold | The final action responds gently and remains visible. |

## Fingerprint plan

- Grammar: editorial confectionery chapters
- Nav: translucent logo bar with orange booking capsule
- Hero: split photographic arch with candy foreground plane
- Sequence: parallax, reveal, pan, full-bleed, stagger, spotlight
- Close: saturated orange booking field plus cream footer
- Signature: sugar trail tied to services progress
- Registry comparison: no existing rows; gate passes.

## Authored silence

- The story section deliberately slows motion.
- The salon image deliberately holds long enough for the atmosphere to register.
